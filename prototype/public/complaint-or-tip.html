Complaint or tip?ቅሬታ ወይስ ጥቆማ?

Both reach the consumer protection office. They are handled differently, so choose the one that fits.

Complaintቅሬታ

Choose this if you bought something and want it put right for you.

  • Rice sold short, or mixed with stones
  • A phone sold as new that was used
  • A kettle that stopped working after three days
  • A service that was paid for and not done

You need an account, what you bought, and the receipt or photos if you have them. The business never sees your name or phone number.

Sign in and startAlready signed in? Start now

Tipጥቆማ

Choose this if you saw a problem in the market that affects many people.

  • Prices above the official price list
  • Goods hidden to push prices up
  • Expired food on sale
  • Scales that give short weight

No account is needed and you can stay anonymous. A tip does not get you a refund, but an officer can follow it up at the market.
