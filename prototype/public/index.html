How it works

From your complaint to an outcomeከቅሬታ እስከ ውጤት

Every complaint gets a number. It goes to the business first, then climbs: Woreda, Sub-City, City Bureau, until it is resolved.

  1. Tell us what happened

    Online, at any time. Add the receipt or photos if you have them.

  2. The business replies

    You and the business talk and can agree an offer, all in one place.

  3. Your Woreda steps in

    If you cannot agree, move it to your Woreda office. Not resolved there, it goes to the Sub-City, then the City Bureau.

  4. You get an outcome

    A refund, a replacement or a repair, recorded with proof.

Know your rights

Three things every shopper should knowሁሉም ሸማች ሊያውቃቸው የሚገቡ ሦስት ነገሮች

Ask for a receipt

A receipt shows what you paid and where. It is the best proof if something goes wrong.

Check the date and the weight

Food past its expiry date and short weights are not allowed. Check before you pay.

Faulty goods can be put right

Goods that do not work as promised can be refunded, replaced or repaired.

Quick answers

Before you startከመጀመርዎ በፊት

Does it cost anything?ክፍያ አለው?

No. A complaint, a tip and the free call line cost nothing.

Will the business know it was me?ድርጅቱ እኔ መሆኔን ያውቃል?

The business sees your complaint but never your name, phone or address.

I have no receiptደረሰኝ የለኝም

You can still complain. Add photos or messages that show the purchase.

What if the business does not answer?ድርጅቱ ባይመልስስ?

Move it to your Woreda trade office. An officer reviews your complaint and helps you settle. If it is not resolved there, it goes to the Sub-City and then the City Bureau.
