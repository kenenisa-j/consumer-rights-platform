How a complaint worksቅሬታ እንዴት ይስተናገዳል

Eight steps, from your complaint to an outcome. You get a message at each important step.

  1. 1. Tell us

    Make a complaint online, or call the free line. You get a number at once.

    Official statuses: Submitted

  2. 2. The business replies

    A registered business gets your complaint first. You and the business can talk and agree an offer.

    Official statuses: With the business

  3. 3. Move it to your Woreda

    If you cannot agree, you move it to the Woreda trade office where you bought. A business that is not on AddisB2B goes straight here.

    Official statuses: Woreda office

  4. 4. Not resolved? The Sub-City

    If the Woreda cannot resolve it, it moves up to the Sub-City trade office.

    Official statuses: Sub-City office

  5. 5. Still not resolved? The City Bureau

    The Addis Ababa City Bureau is the last level. It settles the case or refers it for further action.

    Official statuses: City Bureau

  6. 6. Meetings and visits

    At each level an officer can ask for information, visit the business and bring you together to agree a solution.

    Official statuses: Under Investigation, Under Reconciliation

  7. 7. You agree a solution

    A refund, replacement or repair, recorded with proof.

    Official statuses: Settlement Pending

  8. 8. It is resolved

    When the solution is done, the case is closed.

    Official statuses: Resolved, Closed
