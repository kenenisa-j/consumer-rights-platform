Your rights as a consumerየሸማች መብቶችዎ

What you can expect when you buy goods or services in Addis Ababa, and what we can help with.

Plain-language summary for review

The Directorate's legal office must check this text against the law before it is published. It is not legal advice.

Your rightsመብቶችዎ

To know the price and what you are buyingዋጋውንና የሚገዙትን የማወቅ

The price should be shown before you buy. You should be told the quality, the quantity and, for packaged food, the expiry date.

To buy goods that are safe and not expiredደህንነቱ የተጠበቀና ጊዜው ያላለፈ ዕቃ የመግዛት

Food past its expiry date, and goods that can harm health, must not be sold. Weights and measures must be correct.

To choose freelyበነጻነት የመምረጥ

A seller cannot force you to buy one item to get another, or hide goods to push the price up.

To get a receiptደረሰኝ የማግኘት

Ask for a receipt every time. It shows what you paid, when and where, and it is the best proof if something goes wrong.

To have faulty goods put rightየተበላሸ ዕቃ እንዲስተካከል የመጠየቅ

If goods do not work or are not as described, you can ask for a refund, a replacement or a repair. A service that was paid for should be done as agreed.

To complain and be heardቅሬታ የማቅረብና የመደመጥ

You can make a complaint. It goes to the business first. If you cannot agree, it moves to your Woreda trade office, then the Sub-City, then the City Bureau. Your complaint gets a number and you are told what happens.

Your responsibilitiesኃላፊነቶችዎ

Keep your receipt and the goodsደረሰኙንና ዕቃውን ይያዙ

Do not throw away the item or its packaging until the problem is settled.

Check before you payከመክፈልዎ በፊት ያረጋግጡ

Look at the expiry date, the weight and the price list.

Tell the truthእውነቱን ይናገሩ

Give correct facts in your complaint. False complaints can be closed.

What we can and cannot help with

We can help with

  • Goods and services you bought from a business in Addis Ababa
  • Wrong prices, weights and measures
  • Faulty, expired or unsafe goods
  • Misleading advertising and sales tricks

We cannot decide

  • Rent, housing and land matters
  • Work and salary disputes
  • Disputes between two businesses (on AddisB2B these go through trade disputes)
  • Crimes such as theft or fraud: go to the police

Something went wrong?

Make a complaint and an officer will look at it.
