Questions and answersጥያቄና መልስ

Who can make a complaint?ቅሬታ ማቅረብ የሚችለው ማነው?

Anyone who bought goods or a service from a business in Addis Ababa. You need an account with your phone number.

Does it cost anything?ክፍያ አለው?

No. Making a complaint or a tip is free.

Will the business know it was me?ድርጅቱ እኔ መሆኔን ያውቃል?

The business sees the case number and the facts it needs to reply. It does not see your name, phone, email or address. If you meet to agree a solution, you meet in person at the office.

I have no receipt. Can I still complain?ደረሰኝ የለኝም። ቅሬታ ማቅረብ እችላለሁ?

Yes. Add photos, messages or anything else that shows the purchase. The officer may ask for more.

Which office will handle my complaint?ቅሬታዬን የትኛው ቢሮ ያያል?

The business first. If you cannot agree, your Woreda trade office steps in. Not resolved there, it moves to the Sub-City, and finally to the City Bureau.

Can I get my money back through a tip?በጥቆማ ገንዘቤ ይመለሳል?

No. A tip helps officers act in the market. To get something put right for you, make a complaint.

How is my data used?መረጃዬ እንዴት ይያዛል?

Only consumer protection officers who handle your case can see your details. Businesses never see them.
