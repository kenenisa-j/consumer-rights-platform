Call us for freeበነጻ ይደውሉ

You pay nothing for this call. An officer of the City Bureau answers, writes down your complaint, gives you a case number and sends it to the right office.

8070

Monday to Friday, 8:30 to 17:30

Have these ready

  • What you bought, where and when
  • The name of the business
  • The receipt, if you have it
  • What you want: refund, replacement or repair

Write the 8-character code, for example Q7MD-42KX.
